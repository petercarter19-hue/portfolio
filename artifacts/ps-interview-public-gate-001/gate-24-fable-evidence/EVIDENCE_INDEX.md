## Notes and boundaries

- All 43 exports were rendered from the hash-pinned source above; every
  capture was made with OS reduced-motion signaled, so no export depends on
  animation.
- 200% evidence uses the standard CSS-pixel equivalence (halved viewport).
  At 720 px the desktop grid is intentionally still active (the mobile
  breakpoint is 700 px); the exports demonstrate reflow without loss or
  horizontal page scroll, and the 390 px exports demonstrate the stacked
  behavior beyond the breakpoint.
- The mockup-era footer line "Design authority · no production behavior"
  is intentionally retained in these design exports; it is banned from the
  production implementation (deviation D17).
- Screen-reader names, roles, and live-region behavior are specified
  normatively in `11_REAL_STUDIO_IMPLEMENTATION_ARCHITECTURE.md` §4/§7; a
  static renderer cannot prove announcements, so those remain
  implementation-evidence items (§12) by design.

  product implementation has not started.**
