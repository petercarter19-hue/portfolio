# PeerSlate Constitution

**Version:** v3.0 — lean operating authority
**Status:** Current and owner-directed on 2026-07-31
**Historical detail:** Bible v2.9 remains preserved at
`PeerSlate_Company_and_Product_Bible_v2.9.md`. It is reference material, not
mandatory session reading.

## Purpose

This document is the durable decision card for PeerSlate. It answers what
must not change when product ideas, designs, and implementation choices are
made. It is not a backlog, release checklist, test catalogue, prompt library,
package template, or record of current deployment status.

## Product promise

PeerSlate helps people capture meaningful work and life in their own words,
make it useful when it matters, and deliberately decide what is shared. The
member is the primary beneficiary; public, employer, and collaborator value is
downstream.

## Constitutional rules

1. **Member benefit before engagement.** No popularity, streak, recruiter, or
   application-volume mechanic may displace member usefulness, agency, or calm.
2. **Private before public.** Capture, sources, drafts, interpretations, and
   member intelligence begin private. Sharing, publication, deletion, export,
   and audience changes are explicit member-controlled actions.
3. **Create once, use by reference.** Original input, canonical facts,
   interpretations, and audience-specific projections are separate. Focused
   rooms must not create competing fact stores.
4. **The member remains the authority.** A member can inspect source context,
   correct what the system proposes, and understand what is private, public,
   unavailable, or uncertain.
5. **AI proposes; deterministic software protects trust.** AI never controls
   identity, authorization, publication, deletion, retention, canonical truth,
   or release decisions. Core capture, review, and control remain usable when
   AI or a provider is unavailable.
6. **Authorization happens before retrieval.** Trusted server identity,
   owner/viewer relationship, audience, consent, sensitivity, and purpose are
   resolved before private SQL, search, media, cache, or AI context is returned.
7. **Focused rooms, one connected Slate.** Journal preserves canonical history;
   Studio activates private work; the public Slate presents approved output;
   Community connects deliberately published Community-native social speech
   and selected Slate output. Community-native speech remains authoritative
   only for its own social conversation and does not become canonical Journal
   or Slate fact automatically. A room has one clear purpose and one safe next
   action, not a new navigation forest.
8. **Truthful, accessible craft.** Live, demo, fixture, queued, unavailable,
   and failed states are not confused. Essential paths support text, keyboard,
   assistive technology, zoom, reduced motion, mobile, and recovery.

## Locked boundaries

- PeerSlate is not a job board, recruiter-first marketplace, generic social
  feed, task-management suite, engagement casino, or collection of disconnected
  AI bots.
- Capture is an action, not a required destination. Save Moment creates one
  private canonical Moment; Journal membership is derived and does not copy its
  fact body.
- My Story is a deliberate projection, not the complete private Journal.
- The public Interview Studio retains its current public/browser-local truth
  boundary until a separately approved transition changes it.
- Projects, Story, Work, resume, Studio, and public projections reuse governed
  records; none becomes a second canonical system.

## Decision routing

| If a decision changes… | Update or read… |
| --- | --- |
| Mission, promise, constitutional rule, canonical model, or locked boundary | This Constitution and an owner decision |
| Priority, dependency, or what comes next | Roadmap v3.0 |
| One feature's behavior, data, or acceptance | That task's brief or protected package |
| Design direction | Named visual authority and visual standard |
| Evidence that work is merged, deployed, or live | Release record and control plane |

An ordinary code, copy, test, documentation, or visual-fidelity task does not
need to read this document in full. It cites only the rule it actually touches.
