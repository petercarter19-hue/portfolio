## The path, chapter by chapter

- **The twenties — Pizza, payroll & people.** Before engineering there were
  years managing Domino's Pizza stores — schedules, budgets, and teams under
  pressure. It's where leading people started, long before anyone called it
  leadership.
- **The detour that mattered — running a healthcare company.** Managing a
  healthcare business taught the stakes of getting operations right when
  people depend on you — and left a question that wouldn't go away: what
  else could I be capable of?
- **Back to school at 36 — the bet on myself.** Pete went back to school at
  36 because he wanted goals he had once considered out of reach. Dalton
  State first, then a B.S. in Electrical Engineering at Kennesaw State —
  earned as a non-traditional student while life kept moving. He calls it
  the best decision of his life.
- **2021 — a pandemic hire who learned fast.** Pete joined the Air Force's
  407th Supply Chain Management Squadron as a DoD civilian engineer in the
  middle of COVID — learning a complex engineering job largely over
  telework.
- **2021–2024 — United States Air Force, Robins AFB.** He grew into Lead
  Systems Engineer for navigation-system sustainment and mission-critical
  special projects.
- **2024–today — industry.** Systems effectiveness at L3Harris; systems
  engineering, integration, and test at Northrop Grumman in Huntsville.
  Along the way: a master's in engineering management with a 4.0, and a PMP.
- **January 2027 — the next chapter.** Admitted to the Systems Engineering
  Ph.D. program at the University of South Alabama.
