# PS-RETURN-VALUE-001 — Private Return Value and Member Intelligence

**Status:** Architecture committed; implementation staged and not active

**Owner direction:** July 20, 2026

**Manager/writer for runtime:** Unassigned

**Dependency:** Trustworthy private Journal history, lifecycle, correction,
authorization, and suppression controls

**Runtime truth:** Replay, systematic resurfacing, Momentum, Prompt/Ritual,
What PeerSlate Noticed, and Slate Mirror are not currently implemented by this
package.

## Product purpose

PeerSlate should become more valuable because a member has lived with it—not
because the product manufactures urgency. Return value comes from remembering
something useful, seeing truthful movement, preparing better, noticing a
pattern, completing a meaningful ritual, or finding one worthwhile next step.

## Committed capability families

1. **Replay and resurfacing:** finite, source-linked returns to relevant history.
2. **Momentum:** private recognition of continuity and meaningful movement over
   daily, weekly, project, chapter, seasonal, or member-chosen periods.
3. **Prompt and Ritual Service:** optional, member-controlled prompts and
   recurring practices with quiet hours, pause, snooze, dismiss, and learning.
4. **What PeerSlate Noticed / Slate Mirror:** humble, explainable observations
   over permitted history. This is intended signature quality, not decorative
   AI copy.
5. **Keepsakes and focus:** member-selected references and optional themes that
   make progress personally retrievable without a trophy cabinet.

## Governing line

> You still have the thread. Here is something real from your history, why it
> may matter now, and one optional next step.

## Non-negotiable trust rules

- All return value is private by default.
- Deterministic authorization and eligibility run before retrieval or AI.
- Every insight or comparison cites the exact permitted source Moments/versions.
- The member can inspect, correct, dismiss, mark too personal, suppress a
  source/theme, pause a service, export, and delete applicable state.
- Observations are time-bounded interpretations, not identity, health,
  personality, emotion, skill, or future diagnoses.
- Absence creates no loss. Nothing resets to zero, expires as punishment, or
  shames the member into returning.
- No public streaks, rankings, leaderboards, popularity counts, or comparison.
- A badge/acknowledgement must describe a real criterion and cannot claim an
  unverified competence.
- AI may propose a next step but cannot create, send, publish, place, or change
  canonical records automatically.
- The Journal and core lifecycle remain useful when these services are down.
