# 05 — Citations, counsel gates, and owner decisions

## Research method and boundary

This packet used direct official provider, legal, government, and
provider-controlled documentation pages only.  Search results were used only
to find those pages; no search snippet is evidence.  Pages were accessed on
**2026-08-24**.  No account was created, API called, trial started, provider
contacted, demo requested, term accepted, purchase made, or credential handled.

## Counsel-required questions

Counsel must review the selected agreement and intended data flow before C11 or
C12 begins.  At minimum: whether any display/excerpt/full-description use is
licensed; whether employer-page fact reproduction or an employer-stated-claim
summary is permitted; whether registry/SEC structured fact rendering exceeds
the source terms; whether normalization, embeddings, AI prompts, evaluation,
or model training are a prohibited derivative/secondary use; cache/index/
back-up/log disposition; attribution and trademark treatment; removal SLA;
closure and termination deletion; source-derived tombstone/minimal-fact
survival; cross-border/data-processing terms; indemnity/audit/recordkeeping;
and export/transition obligations.  This package is not legal advice and
offers no legal approval.

## Honest limitation

Official public material is necessarily incomplete for commercial and
employer-source rights.  The ledger therefore uses conservative
unverified/contract/counsel status rather than filling gaps from API capability,
industry practice, source visibility, or unreviewed terms.  The C1
source-class/claim decision permits neither a live claim nor a provider choice;
nothing here proves a provider is suitable, available, affordable, or licensed
for PeerSlate.
