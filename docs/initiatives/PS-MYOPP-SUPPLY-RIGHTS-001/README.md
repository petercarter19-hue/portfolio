# PS-MYOPP-SUPPLY-RIGHTS-001 — C1 supply and rights

**Status:** Protected, documentation-only candidate.  It records a
provider-neutral policy and official-public evidence; it selects no provider,
does not request access, and grants no operational authority.

## Outcome

C1 supplies the decision packet required by parent document 05: a comparison
of representative source classes, candidate `source_policy` rights ledgers, a
provider-neutral connector contract, and the final private-import and
source-linked company-context policy.  It is safe before any physical C2
topology work.  It does **not** authorize a live connector, a source account,
a trial, a contract, spend, a credential, ingestion, schema, index, AI use,
deployment, or enablement.

## Binding C1 policy decisions

1. A shared source is deny-by-default.  It cannot register, acquire, store,
   render, cache, index, or enter an AI prompt until a versioned policy row
   explicitly permits that exact action and surface.
2. Rights are enforced both at registration and every point of use.  A later
   restrictive policy, removal notice, or termination event disables affected
   source content without waiting for re-ingestion.
3. `describe → acquire → normalize` stays provider-neutral.  A connector only
   emits raw receipts and normalized candidates; shared ingestion decides
   admission, canonicalization, lifecycle, and projections.
4. Member paste/upload/import is never a shared receipt, listing, canonical
   opportunity, recommendation input, or discovery-index input.  It remains
   owner-scoped and rights-checked at use.
5. Company context is optional and source-linked.  C1 fixes the permitted
   classes: an employer source may provide only attributable `official_identity`
   / `official_location` facts or visibly labelled `employer_stated_claim`;
   an official registry/SEC source may provide only enumerated identity, filing
   metadata, and structured filing facts with source/`as_of`; a licensed-data
   source has an empty claim allowlist until Pete accepts its exact
   provider/contract packet.  Each source policy separately grants or denies
   the exact claim allowlist, reproduction, and summary right.  It never
   becomes sentiment, reputation, prediction, scoring, or unsupported
   synthesis.
6. Source-derived “minimal facts” are not automatically survivable.  After
   removal or termination, only PeerSlate action records and member-owned
   relationships persist unconditionally; every source-derived identifier,
   title, employer name, date, hash, and provenance detail requires an
   explicit per-source permission.

## What the public evidence establishes

The packet compares two commercial aggregators, a federal public-announcement
API, two employer/ATS job-board interfaces, an employer-owned source example,
and SEC EDGAR company facts.  It uses only direct official pages, dated
2026-08-24.  Public API/interface availability is treated as technical evidence
only, never as a content-rights grant.  The one narrow explicit commercial
example is Adzuna's API terms: conditional publication carries exact
attribution requirements and termination requires removal of acquired data from
web pages.  That is not a current PeerSlate authorization and does not settle
storage, AI, cache, exit, or survivability rights.  See
[01_PROVIDER_COMPARISON.md](01_PROVIDER_COMPARISON.md) and
[02_RIGHTS_LEDGER_CANDIDATES.md](02_RIGHTS_LEDGER_CANDIDATES.md).
