## E. Cross-system review

### Passes

- All 28 PNG exports inside the final ZIP were opened and reviewed at original
  or high detail.
- PUBLIC-03 through PUBLIC-V02 are separate desktop and mobile-portrait exports
  in both themes.
- Light and dark use the same state content and hierarchy rather than separate
  products or dark-only behavior.
- Pete is consistently labeled `Public demo profile`; `Systems Engineer`
  matches the current approved public profile authority.
- The truth strip consistently distinguishes submission, browser storage,
  local media, and non-predictive practice signals.
- No Capture, Moment, Placement, Story, resume edit, sharing, publication,
  account history, or `/app/interview-studio` behavior is invented.
- Mobile content reflows into a readable vertical document instead of scaling
  the desktop frame down.

### Required corrections and missing proof

1. **Exact PUBLIC-01/02 authority is unresolved.** The final package must name
   one exact controlling board/hash or provide the four final separate exports.
   It must also provide their editable source.
2. **The shared shell is inconsistent.** PUBLIC-01/02 boards show the existing
   public/global header plus a Studio-local shell. PUBLIC-03-V02 source replaces
   that with a different PeerSlate/Studio header. The final source must use one
   implementable shared shell without broad global-navigation redesign.
3. **Mobile progress is incomplete.** `source/styles.css` explicitly hides the
   fifth progress step with
   `.progress-line .steps .step:nth-child(n+5){display:none}`. Reflow or scroll;
   do not remove a real step.
4. **Light small gold text misses AA contrast.** The supplied `--gold:
   #b87900` measures 3.43:1 on `#fbf8f2` and 3.60:1 on `#fffefa`. It is used for
   11-pixel eyebrows. Use the approved text-safe gold `#8A5A00` or another
   measured AA treatment for normal text while retaining marigold decoratively.
5. **No-JavaScript evidence is absent.** `source/index.html` has an empty body
   and relies entirely on `app.js`. This is acceptable as a visual renderer,
   but it is not the required server-rendered no-JavaScript truth evidence.
6. **The source is not a semantic/accessibility specification.** Navigation is
   rendered as `div` elements; it has no ARIA/state map, focus design, live
   regions, dialog behavior, screen-reader names, or visible focus variants.
7. **Required responsive evidence is incomplete.** Mobile portrait is present;
   844 x 390 landscape, 200% reflow, long/wrapping content, and translated-label
   stress evidence are absent.
8. **Reduced motion is unspecified.** No `prefers-reduced-motion` treatment or
   non-motion state equivalence is included.
9. **Required unavailable-state evidence is incomplete.** There is no
   JavaScript-unavailable board, local-storage-unavailable History state,
   general media-unavailable state, History empty state, or complete retry and
   recovery sequence.
10. **Component/token/feasibility artifacts are absent.** CSS variables and
    renderer functions provide useful raw source, but there is no shared
    semantic-DOM/component inventory, state map, contrast table, 5A/5C parity
    matrix, current-file mapping, implementation-risk matrix, or approved
    deviation list.
11. **Theme persistence/no-state-loss is unproved.** The static renderer takes a
    query-string theme and replaces `document.body.innerHTML`; it does not map
    to `body[data-theme]` / `ps-theme` or prove preservation of answers, drafts,
    media, focus, caret, dialogs, and scroll. It must not be copied as the
    production theme architecture.
