## Responsive and interaction source

The editable source must include mobile portrait and mobile landscape variants for the primary written, review, video, history, and failure journeys. Annotate:

- hover, visible keyboard focus, selected, disabled, loading, success, and error states;
- 200% zoom/reflow behavior;
- reduced-motion behavior;
- long questions, answers, feedback, and history entries;
- focus movement and restoration for dialogs/disclosures;
- screen-reader names and processing/error announcements;
- local-storage unavailable, JavaScript unavailable, and media unavailable fallbacks.

## Experience rules

- One dominant practice object and one dominant action per state.
- Keep public/demo identity and data-boundary labels visible; do not hide essential truth inside Settings.
- Preserve the visitor's answer during processing and failure.
- Lead review with the bottom line, then What worked, Improve next, framework detail, and secondary actions.
- Any score is secondary and labeled as a practice signal, not an employer prediction.
- Keep advanced setup, Up Next, detailed explanations, and deeper history progressively disclosed.
- Label Interview AI output by source: best-practice, Pete public-profile demo, or comparison.
- Provide an honest camera-off/text-only continuation.
- Do not show feedback before analysis or fabricate processing, history, activity, or video analytics.

## Visual rules

- Use light-first Deep Navy Gold and the semantic allocation in file `05`.
  Image 5 Concept A controls the recognizable default/light composition; Image
  5 Concept C controls the recognizable optional dark composition.
- Both themes use the same information architecture, semantic DOM, component
  placement, actions, functionality, state machine, and accessibility behavior.
- Reuse the existing global `body[data-theme]` / `ps-theme` mechanism in the
  feasibility plan. Theme switching may not reset any Studio state, focus, or
  scroll position.
- Do not use teal as the current step or room-wide active accent.
- Do not import the protected owner navigation rail.
- Preserve the public shell and avoid a broad public-site redesign.
- Keep the experience premium, calm, restrained, accessible, and readable rather than compressing every control above the fold.

## Prohibited current-screen content

Do not place any of the following into a current-public screen as working behavior:

- Sign in to start practicing;
- Create or open my private Studio;
- Use my private history;
- Save as Interview Story;
- server-backed History & Growth;
- job-description upload or analysis;
- per-member entitlements or paid tiers;
- protected owner identity or navigation;
- uploaded video, transcript processing, or delivery analytics;
- a fictional zero-input tour as the only public experience.
