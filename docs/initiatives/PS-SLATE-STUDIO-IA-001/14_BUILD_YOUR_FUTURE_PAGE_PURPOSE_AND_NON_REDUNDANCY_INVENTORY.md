## Scope

- **Member need:** privately name a future, make a low-pressure next move, and
  decide whether a future goal has become useful enough to complete or propose
  elsewhere.
- **Surface:** future-facing Goal Board inside Build Your Future.
- **Category contract:** Short Term, Long Term, Work, Custom. Custom requires
  a member label.
