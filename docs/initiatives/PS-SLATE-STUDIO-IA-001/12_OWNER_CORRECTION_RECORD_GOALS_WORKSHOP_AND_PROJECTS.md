## 1. Build Your Future Goal Board

The Goal Board is a future-facing private workspace. It is **not** work history,
a career-experience card system, a project tracker, or another canonical truth
store.

Its starting category contract is exactly:

- Short Term
- Long Term
- Work
- Custom

`Custom` asks the member for a label. New goals begin through a small **Add
Goal** prompt. A goal may have an optional soft ETA, private background, and an
explicit optional Feed-sharing choice. It remains private by default. Any Feed
projection is a separate future, member-authorized action; a Goal never
automatically becomes Feed, Story, résumé-page, or canonical-record content.

The future product vocabulary may include Voice, Ask Slate, progress, supportive
Sparks, Try Another Future, and Small Experiments. Those are not current
runtime commitments, and AI continues to propose while the member decides.
Small Experiments are low-risk, optionally time-bounded ways to learn - not
task management, a failure state, or a pressure system.

The following are rejected for this Goal Board direction: work-history records,
career-experience cards, Projects as a fixed category, connecting lines, zoom
controls, arrange controls, current-focus markers, file uploads, automatic
publication, automatic résumé/Story/Feed/canonical updates, pressure, streaks,
punitive deadlines, and failure framing. Fullscreen is deferred pending an
approved purpose and visual decision.

Two future Goal Board concepts may be created by ChatGPT after the V0/V1
page-purpose gate is complete and Pete approves the inventory. No existing
