**Date:** 2026-07-22; reconciled with Pete's 2026-07-23 refinements.
**Author:** Claude Code (program manager); 2026-07-23 owner refinements
reconciled by Codex at Pete's direction. **Method:** every idea from the three tracked inputs
(owner/Claude brainstorm, ChatGPT brainstorm, adopted planning instructions)
and the later owner refinements is merged **by the job it performs**, not by
name, per the owner-approved merge rule. Each canonical system lists its
alternate brainstorm names, then is scored against the six tests from the
planning instructions (Tuesday, Grounded, Pocket, Plumbing, Clarity, Scope)
and classified: **Lock / First-wave candidate / Strong later / Revisit
Register / Merge / Reject-standalone / Reject.**

Convergence note: ideas proposed independently by more than one source are
marked ★ (validation, not authorship credit).

  PS-STORY-COMPOSER-001's future gate. The existing public Résumé and My Story

## 4. Practice and coaching engine ★ — communication reps with a real feedback loop

- **Merges:** Interview Studio (live), Rehearsal Room, Explain It, The Pitch
  Clock, Hard Conversations Gym, Story Rehearsal, Question Shuffle (practice
  aspect), Train Your Twin (practice aspect), Coaching Studio, Review Studio,
  Presentation Studio, Practice Studio, and Communication Studio.
- **Job:** one private practice, rehearsal, review, and coaching system —
  scenario families × audience × role × stakes × time constraint, using the
  member's permitted real material when authorized. Interviews are one family,
  alongside presentations and pitches, explanations, performance and promotion
  conversations, constructive feedback, and other difficult workplace
  conversations. A kept take may become an optional private Moment.
- **Tests:** Tuesday ✓ (the five-minute rep) · Grounded ✓ (its moat: practice
  against your actual record) · Pocket ✓ · Plumbing — public Interview
  Studio is **live**; signed-in persistence + member grounding are new;
  scenario families and a tailored feedback loop require separately governed
  prompt/API, safety, data, and visual work; they are not separate products ·
  Clarity ✓ ("Practice") · Scope ✓ (families are modes — 4.7 rule).
- **Required feedback loop:** establish the situation, audience, role, and
  constraint → let the member practice → return specific constructive feedback
  → support retry/comparison → let the member explicitly keep or discard the
  result. No employability grade, personality diagnosis, predicted outcome,
  autonomous HR decision, or replacement for legal/policy/human judgment.
- **Disposition: LOCK as the target product direction; transition staged.**
  The current public Interview Studio remains unchanged and keeps its current
  name until a separate discovery, naming, architecture, truth/safety, visual,
  homepage-parity, and implementation package passes. Pitch Clock and Hard
  Conversations Gym = **Reject-standalone, Merge** as scenario families.
  Coaching, Review, Presentation, Practice, and Communication Studio are naming
  candidates for one umbrella—not parallel products. Train Your Twin's
  public-AI-QA aspect remains a **strong later** feature of the Ask [Name] AI
  boundary with an explicit approve-to-public flow.

## 11. Acceptance frames carried into D6

- **The two-journey test** ★: Purposeful ("I came to improve my résumé") and
  Exploratory ("no plan, found something worth working on") must both feel
  natural, or the Studio is a reorganized toolbox, not a sandbox.
- **The twenty-minute test:** new member with a PDF résumé → presentable
  live Slate in ~20 minutes without speaking to AI once; the same journey
  speak-only. Both first-class (doc 14).
- **The Tuesday loop:** five minutes, zero agenda, leave with something in
  your pocket — or with nothing changed and no penalty.
- **The practice-feedback test:** the broader practice system must help a
  member understand what worked, what could improve, and what to try next, then
  support a retry without grading the person or making the underlying
  professional decision for them.
- **The sandbox promise** (verbatim, on every draft surface): *"Nothing you
  try here changes what is live until you decide."*
