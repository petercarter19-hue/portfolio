## Controlling visual decisions

- Real primary navigation: **Pete's Slate · Community · Interview Studio ·
  Workshop**. Workshop sits next to Interview Studio; it does not replace it.
- Workshop is private by default and builds the member's controlled information
  library. AI proposes; the member reviews and explicitly saves.
- Member source wording, PeerSlate interpretation/proposal, confirmed private
  information, and downstream use remain visibly distinct.
- Exact final wording is directly reviewable before **Save privately**.
- `Save unfinished` is a private working draft, not confirmed information.
- Saving privately does not publish, share, or silently change another
  PeerSlate destination.
- PeerSlate does not create resume documents. Future downstream actions may
  update existing PeerSlate resume-page content, My Story, or Feed only after
  explicit member choice. These destinations are shown as **Coming later** in
  the current visual direction.
- Work / Personal / Both is an organizational classification, not an audience
  or privacy control.
- Voice uses one familiar composer: ordinary text field, inline microphone,
  and an enabled/disabled submit arrow where the field itself submits. There
  are no Type/Speak tabs.
- Long-form editors include an inline microphone but keep their existing
  screen-level Save or Review action; they do not add another submit arrow.
- Voice is present wherever members author meaningful text, including direct
  Workshop entry, questions, search, and edits. It is absent from filters,
  navigation, and read-only information.
- Text entry remains available when voice is recording, transcribing, fails,
  or lacks microphone permission.
- The visual system uses dark neutral text for headings/body and reserves
  cobalt for actions, links, focus, and selection. Carry the professional
  typography, spacing, alignment, and information density through every
  responsive and functional state.
- Role/Qualification Alignment remains a separate focused product surface.
  Workshop may strengthen missing evidence, but it does not become a job-fit
  scoring or job-board workflow.
