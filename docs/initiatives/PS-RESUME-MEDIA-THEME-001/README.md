# PS-RESUME-MEDIA-THEME-001 - Resume media composition and theme continuity

**Status:** Planned - not active.
**Authority placement:** Fresh visual follow-up to
`PS-RESUME-PUBLIC-REFINE-001`; it does not overwrite that released authority
without a new accepted visual lock.
**Runtime status:** No template, image, upload, theme, or public-page change is
authorized.

## Owner outcome

Replace the awkward two-image/quote sandwich with one predictable photograph
and an optional quote. Carry the warm Overview visual family into the formal
Resume and left rail. Give future members a small, coherent library of generic
images for My Philosophy and What's Next.

## Composition direction

- Use one primary landscape image slot with a controlled crop ratio.
- Make the quote optional and place it above or below the image rather than
  requiring two complementary photographs around it.
- Preserve one warm foundation, typography, rule, forest/navy/bronze language,
  and left-rail family from Overview into the formal Resume.
- The formal Resume may become denser and more structured without looking like
  a different product.

## Curated image library

Test a finite starting library of roughly 18-24 intentionally licensed and
labeled choices across Purpose, Leadership, Growth, What's Next, and Abstract.
Use a consistent crop/focal-point contract. Avoid an unlimited stock search,
unclear licensing, and images that imply a member's real experience.

Every generic image is illustrative and optional. A later member-media phase
may add **Upload my own** only after reusable upload, crop, focal point,
ownership, deletion, moderation, and source contracts are ready.

## Delivery phases

1. **Public composition/theme:** lock and implement the one-image layout,
   optional quote, and color continuity using already approved assets.
2. **Reusable member picker:** add curated choices and member uploads through a
   protected profile/media package; never add Pete-specific product logic.

## Acceptance gate

Create a new ChatGPT visual direction and Pete acceptance covering image/quote
variants, missing image, long quote, focal crop, generic-image labeling,
alt-text workflow, mobile/tablet/desktop, and continuity at the Overview-to-
Resume transition. Record asset license/provenance before release.
