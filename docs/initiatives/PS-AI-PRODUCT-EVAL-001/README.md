# PS-AI-PRODUCT-EVAL-001 - AI product quality, model, prompt, and evaluation program

**Status:** Planned - not active. Documentation and decision charter only.
**Owner:** Pete.
**Manager / writer / branch:** Unassigned.
**Runtime status:** No runtime work, model selection, provider change, prompt
deployment, data access, feature enablement, or live AI claim is authorized.

## Purpose

This cross-product initiative turns the future AI discussion into one durable
decision program rather than scattered feature prompts. It defines how PeerSlate
will decide whether AI support is actually helpful, grounded, privacy-safe, in
the member's voice, and ready to release.

It covers public Ask Pete / reusable Ask [Name], signed-in Ask Slate, interview
coaching, future Workshop and Build Your Future assistance, résumé-page and
Story proposals, Projects, return-value interpretation, and any later
AI-assisted practice workflow. It does not replace their bounded packages.

## Controlling principles

- AI proposes; a member decides. AI never silently saves, edits, completes,
  shares, publishes, sends, deletes, or becomes canonical truth.
- Deterministic server software resolves identity, authorization, ownership,
  audience, sources, lifecycle, and release controls before any model request
  or protected retrieval.
- The member's natural voice, uncertainty, and source language are preserved;
  AI does not turn ordinary speech into unsupported corporate performance copy.
- A model/provider is selected through recorded task evaluation, not permanently
  hardcoded because of a brand name or a single demonstration.
- Automated judging assists review; it does not replace human judgment about
  member value, tone, truthfulness, or harm.

## Sequence and gate

The major owner discussion occurs after Workshop and Build Your Future direction
are settled and after Projects has a defined purpose, but before broad Ask Slate
behavior or advanced coaching is designed, selected, or released. A narrow
existing public Ask Pete service remains governed by its released public-source
boundary; this initiative does not expand it.

Entry requires a named manager, one bounded workflow, current source and
authorization contracts, page-purpose inventory/visual authority where member
visible, a human-reviewed golden case set, a provider/model evaluation plan,
and explicit owner approval of the particular launch threshold. Each runtime
workflow then receives its own package, writer, evidence, acceptance, and
release gate.
