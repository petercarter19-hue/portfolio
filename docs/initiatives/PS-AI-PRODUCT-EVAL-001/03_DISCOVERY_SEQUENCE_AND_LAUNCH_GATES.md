# Discovery sequence, roles, and launch gates

## Owner discussion sequence

1. Finish and settle Workshop purpose and Build Your Future/Goal Board purpose.
2. Hold the Projects purpose discussion and define how Projects differs from
   Work, Goals, Story, and evidence.
3. Convene the AI product discussion using the inventory and interview contract
   in this package: desired coaching voice, follow-up behavior, good-answer
   examples, no-evidence language, member controls, sources, privacy, and what
   should never be automated.
4. Build and approve an initial human-reviewed golden case set and task-specific
   evaluation scorecard.
5. Run a bounded provider/model evaluation and select a candidate for one
   narrow workflow.
6. Create a separate architecture/implementation package for that workflow;
   only then consider broad Ask Slate or advanced coaching behavior.

## Roles and agents

- **Pete:** chooses the product promise, desired voice, unacceptable behavior,
  representative cases, and launch threshold. Pete approves any material visual
  authority and the eventual member-visible behavior.
- **Designated manager:** protects scope, source/authorization boundaries,
  shared records, evaluation evidence, and honest claims.
- **Architect (when required):** establishes the bounded data, privacy,
  retrieval, provenance, and failure architecture once; it does not invent the
  desired coaching experience.
- **Writer:** implements the accepted bounded workflow, tests it, self-reviews
  it, and records exact model/prompt/evaluation evidence.
- **Independent reviewer (risk-triggered):** reviews the exact candidate where
  consequential AI, private data, authorization, publication, or another
  mandatory trigger applies.
- **Human evaluators:** review golden cases and representative outputs; their
  product judgment is not substituted by automated judging.

Use the current `docs/AI_MODEL_AND_ROLE_ROUTING.md` for delivery roles and
available model aliases. Do not embed a permanent product model choice in this
initiative.

## Launch stop conditions

Do not launch a workflow when any of these remain unresolved:

- source authorization occurs after retrieval, an AI service can see another
  member's material, or the source set is not inspectable;
- output can silently write, complete, share, publish, delete, or become
  canonical truth;
- golden cases lack owner/human review, adversarial/privacy coverage, or a
  regression baseline;
- no-evidence, contradiction, unavailable provider, malformed output, or
  member correction behavior is unknown;
- the candidate's benefit, voice preservation, latency, or cost threshold is
  not owner-approved; or
- a member-visible surface lacks its V0/V1 page-purpose inventory and required
  visual authority.

## Eventual decision record

The first runtime package must record the evaluated candidates, exact versions,
golden-case results, rejected/accepted failure findings, selected workflow,
source policy, model/provider decision and rationale, latency/cost envelope,
member correction design, rollback/disable control, owner acceptance, and the
separate status of implementation, merge, deployment, enablement, and live
verification.
