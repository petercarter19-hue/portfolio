# 14 — Owner Review Checklist

This is the final plain-language review Pete can use after Codex supplies technical evidence.

## First-viewport test

At 1440 × 900 and 1366 × 768, confirm without scrolling:

- the current question is unmistakable;
- the large textarea is the obvious primary input;
- `Use dictation` is available but visually secondary;
- save/word status is near the answer;
- `Review My Answer` is near the answer and is the dominant next action;
- session setup and supporting information are present but quieter;
- the page is white, not beige or cream.

## Flow test

Walk through:

1. open Interview Me;
2. type an answer;
3. reload and verify the draft behavior remains truthful;
4. open and close Up Next;
5. edit session settings using the current behavior;
6. use optional dictation, then continue typing in the same answer;
7. submit for coaching;
8. watch processing without losing the answer;
9. review feedback;
10. improve the answer;
11. retry or continue;
12. trigger or simulate coaching failure and verify the complete answer is safe.

The flow should feel like one workspace changing state, not several pages stacked on top of one another.

## Supporting destinations

Confirm:

- Interview AI uses the same shell and visual system without losing its current modes or grounding behavior;
- Video Practice remains local and does not imply delivery analytics or upload;
- History remains browser-local and does not imply account sync;
- History is visually a destination, not a fourth practice method.

## Mobile test

At 390 × 844 and 320 × 568:

- textarea remains the primary object;
- keyboard does not hide the active line or next action;
- question context remains available through a compact sticky summary;
- action dock respects the safe area and never covers content;
- Up Next/settings/privacy use accessible sheets or drawers;
- review and improve states remain understandable without desktop side-by-side compression.

## Theme and color test

Light theme:

- white page foundation;
- white cards/surfaces;
- cool blue-gray support areas;
- navy text;
- cobalt selected/action/focus treatment;
- teal only for state/success/listening accents;
- no beige, cream, ivory, tan, parchment, gold, or amber foundation.

Dark theme:

- same structure, controls, copy order, and actions as light;
- no missing feature or alternate dark-only workflow.

## Truth and safety test

Verify the interface does not claim:

- audio or video upload;
- account-backed history;
- cross-device sync;
- employer prediction;
- automatic publication;
- private Slate data in the public demo;
- video delivery analysis that does not exist.

## Approval decision

Approve only when all are true:

- no functionality was removed;
- no new product architecture was invented;
- typing is primary and dictation is optional;
- the question and answer controls stay together;
- future states wait until relevant;
- white/cobalt visual direction is consistent;
- desktop, mobile, light, dark, failure, and permission states are proven;
- Codex supplies tests, screenshots, diff summary, rollback, and no-merge/no-deploy statement.
