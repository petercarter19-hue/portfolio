## C. What this means in plain English

PeerSlate now has one coherent product rule ready to become repository
authority: a person can capture inspiration wherever they are, press Save
Moment once, and find that same private Moment in their complete Journal. They
can later use the exact Moment in Story, résumé, Work, Projects, Feed, Studio,
or another supported purpose without copying the facts or passing through an
artificial Add to Journal gate.

The Journal is the owner's complete working memory. My Story remains the
finite, deliberately composed explanation a person chooses to present. Public
and Connection views never expose the private Journal and filter it in the
browser; the server constructs only the exact authorized projection.

## E. How this connects to PeerSlate

The update turns “one connected Slate” into an enforceable reference model.
Canonical Moment truth stays private-first and member-owned. Journal provides
complete chronology and retrieval. Purpose-specific rooms consume authorized
exact-version references. My Story is authored composition rather than a
second fact store. Return services bring useful history back without punitive
engagement mechanics. Ask Slate reasons over only authorized context and cannot
silently edit or publish. This keeps the Deep Navy Gold visual quality bar,
multi-user/tenant rules, evidence boundaries, and separate public/private
states intact while leaving exact navigation open.
