## What remains valid

The update does not weaken these controls:

- Original source and member language remain preserved according to lifecycle
  policy.
- A Moment is the canonical member-owned record; purpose-specific presentation
  never silently forks its facts.
- AI output remains a proposal and never controls identity, authorization,
  audience, publication, deletion, retention, or sending.
- Authorization occurs before retrieval. Unauthorized payloads omit private
  records, counts, media metadata, intelligence, and relationship clues.
- New content remains Only Me unless the member explicitly selects another
  permitted audience and completes the relevant publication/share action.
- Text and Speak remain first-class, with an accessible text fallback.
- Deep Navy Gold, visual-integrity, accessibility, cross-product homepage
  parity, self-managed writers, Azure authority, and exact evidence remain
  binding.
- Projects remain planned and private-first; the update does not silently start
  Projects, Story Composer, public Journal, messaging, or Ask Slate runtime work.

## Compatibility interpretation for released backend packages

The released Capture, Moment, and Placement packages are reusable foundations,
not discarded work.

1. A global composer will orchestrate existing source/versioning services and
   the Moment save boundary behind one member action.
2. “Save Moment” is explicit member confirmation of the member-authored content
   being saved. AI enrichment may follow; it cannot replace that version.
3. Derived Journal membership performs no downstream write, so it does not
   violate the released rule that Moment confirmation does not create a
   Placement or destination copy.
4. Existing `/app/capture` and legacy Journal endpoints require a separately
   tested compatibility/retirement plan. Governance does not pretend they have
   already changed.
5. Migration must preserve every owner, source, revision, Moment version,
   archive/delete state, and audit relationship. No bulk auto-publication or
   inferred audience is allowed.
