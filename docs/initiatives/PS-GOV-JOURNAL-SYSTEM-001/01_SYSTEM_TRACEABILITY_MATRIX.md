## Owner-decision coverage check

The following concerns are explicitly covered and may not be dropped by a
downstream implementation brief:

- Capture is available from Home, Journal, Story/Story Composer, Work, résumé
  creation, Studio, Board, Projects when active, and other eligible signed-in
  contexts.
- The origin room can provide optional context and suggested reuse, but it
  cannot force a destination choice before Save Moment.
- Saving closes the universal composer or returns the member to their original
  context; it does not route them through Journal unless they choose to open it.
- Journal owner, curated, and audience views are views of one record system.
- Work/Personal/Both is an independent lens, not a visibility or audience.
- Importance, curation, placement, audience, and publication are independent
  metadata/decisions.
- Public Journal may feel like a profile/timeline; My Story remains the more
  authored, visually composed narrative.
- Messaging is committed but may be sequenced after the first Journal release.
- Momentum may use weekly, project, chapter, seasonal, or member-chosen periods.
- Purposeful badges acknowledge something real; they never assert an unverified
  skill or create social rank.
- What PeerSlate Noticed and Slate Mirror are signature quality, not decorative
  AI copy. They require enough history, explainability, member correction, and
  careful language.
- Life Constellation, worldbuilding, synthetic own-voice playback, and the
  remaining hook inventory stay findable without becoming current scope.
