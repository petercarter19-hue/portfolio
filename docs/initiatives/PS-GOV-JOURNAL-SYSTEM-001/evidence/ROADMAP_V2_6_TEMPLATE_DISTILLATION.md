# Roadmap v2.6 Template Distillation

## Control purpose

This record proves that Roadmap v2.7 preserves the approved Roadmap v2.6
document system while changing the phase/package sequence required by the new
Journal authority. It is not a redesign of the Roadmap or a claim that planned
services are already live.

## Template characteristics preserved

- Five US Letter sections in the existing orientation sequence: portrait,
  landscape, portrait, landscape, portrait.
- Existing margins, header/footer linkage, first-page behavior, and section
  boundaries.
- One inline image and its relationship/media byte.
- All 119 tables and their geometry.
- Styles, theme, numbering definitions, relationships, and media.
- The 23-member DOCX package structure.
- Existing field architecture: 208 `PAGEREF` fields and one `PAGE` field.

- `docProps/core.xml`;

## Deliberate v2.7 changes

- Current version, authority, supersession, and running-identity text now name
  Roadmap v2.7.
- Phase 5 starts with universal in-context Capture, one `Save Moment`, one
  private canonical Moment, and immediate derived owner-Journal retrieval.
- Capture destination, private-draft promotion, reviewed-Moment placement, and
  Journal-hold assumptions are superseded.
- Private Journal foundation precedes dependent return/intelligence work, but
  the roadmap does not create a false single serial train: bounded Return and
  owner-only Ask Slate can start after the private foundation without waiting
  for public Journal.
- Public/selected Journal projection, My Story composition, messaging, Photo
  enablement, Home compatibility, and Projects keep their separate gates.
- `Use This Moment` has a complete accessible chooser in addition to finite
  shortcuts.
- PS-RETURN-VALUE-001, PS-ASK-SLATE-AI-001, and PS-MESSAGING-001 receive
  explicit planned status, activation prerequisites, and non-claims.
- Appendix H carries implementation allocations, tests, rollout boundaries,
  legal gates, revisit items, and role-routing rules.

Heading counts changed from H1/H2/H3 `34/186/0` to `35/191/7`. Two semantic
data-table headers were added, changing marked header rows from 44 to 46. The
remaining unmarked presentation/layout tables are reviewed in the consolidated
verification record.

## Inherited formatting debt

The style linter reports 1,236 directly formatted runs and 1,548 directly
formatted paragraphs. These are inherited template characteristics. The first
heading-like warnings are cover and TOC display lines, not body headings that
were stripped of their semantic style. Normalizing all inherited formatting
would be a separate document-modernization change and is outside this bounded
authority package.

## Distillation result

Roadmap v2.7 retains the v2.6 visual and structural system while making the
owner-approved architecture executable as staged package gates. Planned work
remains planned; no route, schema, flag, deployment setting, or member-facing
service is made live by the document.
