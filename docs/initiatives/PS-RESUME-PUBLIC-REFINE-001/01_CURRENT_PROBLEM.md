# PS-RESUME-PUBLIC-REFINE-001 — Current Problem and Intent

## What is already real

The public résumé is live at `/petec/resume`. It has an opening identity/portrait, section ribbon, Ask Pete AI, impact evidence, skills evidence, experience chapters, credentials, Career Constellation, contact path, and résumé/PDF action. Its tests already protect canonical routing, data-driven rendering, chapters, skills, credentials, and constellation integration.

## Problem to solve

The current page repeats hierarchy and actions in the opening, then presents several full overview layers before the visitor reaches deeper career navigation. The result is a long default scan even though many details already have interactive disclosure behavior.

The refinement should make the reading path easier to understand at a glance:

- Who is this person and what is the professional through-line?
- What impact and capabilities are most important?
- Where can I inspect the evidence and chronology?
- How do I ask a grounded question, contact them, or obtain the traditional résumé?

## Design intent

- One dominant résumé object and clearer visual hierarchy in the opening viewport.
- Fewer repeated labels/actions; no loss of meaning.
- Progressive depth: strong summary first, evidence and chapter detail on deliberate request.
- Generous, readable document flow rather than compressing everything above the fold.
- Newsreader for cinematic/editorial headings and Inter for navigation, controls, forms, metadata, and product content.
- Preserve Deep Navy Gold and the existing résumé’s purpose rather than inventing a new visual system.

The Roadmap’s 8–9% target is a perceived desktop compression goal, not permission to shrink text, obscure information, or delete approved content.
