## Why this Protected activation is required

Constitution v3.0 rule 7 says Community connects selected output. The current
Journal package also says direct Feed creation does not bypass Save Moment.
Those statements correctly protect Slate-derived fact reuse, but without a
narrow clarification they also appear to exclude the newer owner-approved
Community-native social origin.

This package aligns the durable constitutional rule and the exact Journal reuse
contract. It does not weaken the one-Moment architecture. It distinguishes new
social speech from reuse of an existing member fact.

## Activated contract

- Community-native content is authoritative only for its Community post and
  conversation.
- A draft remains private; publication is an explicit member action to an
  allowed audience and remains subject to server-derived identity,
  authorization, moderation, revocation, and deletion contracts.
- Community-native content never creates or mutates a Moment, Journal record,
  public Slate fact, résumé, Story, Work, Project, or other canonical member
  fact automatically.
- A Feed item that reuses a Slate fact is still a governed exact-version
  projection/reference and cannot bypass Save Moment.
- Converting or relating Community-native speech to a Moment later requires a
  separate previewed member action and separately approved contract.
- AI cannot post, publish, widen audience, save to Journal, connect a source,
  or establish canonical truth.
