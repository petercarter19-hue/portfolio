## 1. Shared rules for every connective pattern

These apply to all six patterns and are not restated in each section.

1. **A pattern is not a destination.** Each lives inside an existing room. None
   adds a global navigation entry, and none may become a top-level page.
2. **Server-authorized payload only.** The pattern renders a
   `ResolvedRoomContext` produced by the server. It never queries arbitrary
   data, and private context is never sent to the browser to be filtered there.
   See `03_ARCHITECTURE_AND_DATA_STANDARD_CONNECTED_SYSTEM.md`.
3. **Reference, never copy.** A pattern references canonical records or exact
   governed versions. It never copies authoritative facts into a second
   room-specific truth.
4. **Connective budget.** One primary action and no more than two secondary
   actions per state.
5. **Do not interrupt the task.** Orientation appears before the task;
   continuation appears after meaningful progress. Neither appears mid-task.
6. **Empty is a valid render.** When no truthful relationship exists, the
   pattern renders nothing. Fabricating a relationship is a defect, not a
   fallback.
7. **Truthful status.** Every state states honestly what is live, stored,
   transmitted, browser-local, private, public, illustrative, or future.
8. **Dismissible and non-diagnostic.** Temporal and AI-derived cues can always
   be dismissed, corrected, or marked not useful. None may diagnose the person,
   their mood, their capability, or their future.
9. **No pressure.** No streak, loss framing, guilt, punitive recovery,
   artificial scarcity, public consistency, or popularity signal.
10. **Accessibility and failure parity.** Every pattern must pass mobile,
    keyboard, screen reader, visible focus, 200-percent zoom, reduced motion,
    long content, empty, restricted, stale, deleted-source, provider-failure,
    error, retry, and recovery states, and must not slow the primary task.

## 2. Slate Spine

**Purpose:** orient a member inside a focused room without creating another
navigation bar.

**Required elements**

- Room role in one concise line.
- Viewer / audience or truth label when material.
- Zero to two meaningful relationships.
- One dominant next action.
- Optional status or limitation statement.

**Placement**

- Near the hero or primary content header.
- May collapse on mobile into a compact contextual card.
- Must not compete with the room's primary task.

**States**

public approved relationship; owner private relationship; no relationship
available; source deleted or unavailable; action unavailable or `Coming later`;
browser-local limitation; restricted audience; AI unavailable.

**Open:** the exact visual form and the canonical name of this pattern are open
owner decisions. "Slate Spine" is a working label.

## 3. Resume Backstory Drawer

**Use only for three to five anchor claims initially.** Which claims is an open
owner decision.

**Content hierarchy**

1. What the claim says.
2. How it was earned: context, contribution, outcome.
3. Related Project, Story chapter, or approved supporting context.
4. Optional "Practice telling this" action.
5. Source or provenance language appropriate to the audience.

**Rules**

- Public-safe only on the public Resume.
- No raw private Journal, owner notes, or source files.
- The drawer is progressive depth, not default bulk. The Resume's refined
  progressive default scan must not regress.
- The practice route must preserve the exact live Studio truth: the public
  Studio is browser-local practice, not account-backed history.

**Open:** whether public Studio support is curated configuration or
projection-backed after canonical services mature.

## 4. Studio Return Ticket

**Trigger only after meaningful progress**, such as Review or Improve. Never
during an active answer.

**Public / browser-local version**

- Primary: reconnect to the approved work behind the answer.
- Secondary: practice a related question.
- Secondary: review the browser-local attempt where current behavior supports
  it.

**Owner future version** (not designed, not authorized)

- Save what changed in how I explain this.
- Connect practice to a confirmed Moment or Project.
- Try again later with a source-linked variation.
- Compare with an earlier owner-authorized attempt.

**Rules**

- Never interrupt the active answer flow.
- Never claim server persistence in public / browser-local mode.
- Never save, connect, or schedule without an explicit member action.

**Open:** the exact owner-mode Return Ticket actions and their storage.

## 5. Then and Now

**Inputs**

- Two or more authorized, source-linked records.
- A deterministic time or relationship basis.
- Optional AI wording, only after sources are established.

**Output examples**

- Same challenge, different season.
- You described this differently six months ago.
- This explanation contains more specific context than the earlier version.
- An earlier decision contributed to this outcome.

**Controls**

inspect sources; not related; too personal; not useful; dismiss; correct
relationship; use this Moment.

**Rules**

- Deterministic relationship basis first; AI may only word the observation.
- Never a claim about identity, capability, mental state, or future.
- Requires mature confirmed history, source authorization, contradiction
  handling, and correction behavior before implementation.

## 6. Focus Themes

A Focus Theme is an **optional private overlay**, not a destination and not an
identity label.

**Examples**

Explain my work clearly. Practice calm confidence. Reclaim momentum. Prepare for
a leadership transition. Make technical contribution visible. Build toward a
chosen learning goal.

**May**

tune prompts, resurfacing, Replay, Studio question selection, and one next step.

**May not**

silently filter out other history; define the person; become public without an
explicit projection; persist as a label on any public payload.

**Open:** whether Focus Themes are fixed, member-authored, AI-suggested, or
hybrid.

## 7. Progress Keepsakes

A Keepsake is a **reference to an existing authorized object or projection**.

**Candidates**

strongest interview clip; an exact human sentence; a confirmed Resume claim; a
before-and-now pair; a Replay Moment; a Story chapter; a meaningful Project
outcome.

**Rules**

- No copied authoritative text as a new truth.
- No points, rarity, ranking, or public trophy behavior.
- Deleting or revoking the source updates or removes the Keepsake.

**Open:** whether Keepsakes live inside Replay, My Slate, Story, or a private
collection view.

## 8. Content voice for connective copy

Extends the Bible's existing content-voice rules; it does not replace them.

**Aligned**

- "Welcome back. Nothing was lost."
- "This answer is more specific than the earlier version."
- "Same challenge, different season."
- "This result supports a story you may want to practice."
- "One small contribution is enough today."
- "This connects to a Focus Theme you chose."
- "See how this line was earned."
- "Return to the Moment behind this answer."
- "You still have the thread."

**Not aligned**

- "Do not lose your streak."
- "You are falling behind."
- "Explore more." / "You may also like."
- Any phrasing that praises, diagnoses, ranks, or implies obligation.
