## Locked product contract

1. **Journal is the memory profile.** It is the member's chronological,
   private-first home and the default Journal view of the same connected Slate
   for permitted viewers.
2. **Moment remains canonical.** Capture preserves original source input; a
   reviewed, confirmed Moment holds member-approved canonical meaning. Journal
   does not copy that narrative into a second truth store.
3. **One person, one Slate, several authorized views.** Owner, selected-person,
   Connection, member, and Public experiences resolve on the server. Private
   records are excluded from unauthorized payloads.
4. **Member Intelligence is private interpretation.** “What PeerSlate
   noticed,” Slate Mirror, and Replay are source-linked, uncertain,
   correctable, dismissible, exportable, and deletable. They never silently
   become profile facts or public claims.
5. **Activation is explicit.** Use This Moment may propose a private draft,
   placement, content link, reminder, Goal/Project step, or Studio scenario.
   The member approves the action; no automatic publication or audience
   broadening is allowed.
6. **Feed is downstream.** Community may render an authorized Journal
   projection later. It does not own a duplicate post body.
7. **AI failure is survivable.** Journal list, detail, source inspection,
   correction, archive, export, and deletion remain usable without AI.
