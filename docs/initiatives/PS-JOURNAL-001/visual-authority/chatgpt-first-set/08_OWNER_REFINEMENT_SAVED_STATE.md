## Do not have ChatGPT design My Story (owner note, 2026-07-21)

A polished My Story page already exists and is the visual authority. **ChatGPT
must not create, redesign, or mock up a My Story page** — inventing one risks a
divergent, lower-quality version that fights the real page and violates the
Visual Integrity Standard ("the real product is upstream authority").

For this Journal set, ChatGPT designs only the **new** surfaces: the Journal,
the universal composer, and the saved state. When an image needs an "origin
room" behind the pop-out, use the **Journal or a calm Home**, or attach a
**screenshot of the real My Story page** as a static backdrop — never a
re-invented one.

## What changes, and what does not

- **JOURNAL-01, 02, 03 are unchanged.** JOURNAL-02 is the composer while the
  member is *writing* — correctly it shows no destinations yet.
- **JOURNAL-04 is adjusted:** keep its purpose (the deliberately minimal saved
  state that proves a save does *not* auto-insert into a destination), but
  **change its origin from a mocked-up My Story to the Journal or Home**. If a
  My Story origin is genuinely wanted, attach a screenshot of the real page as a
  static backdrop instead of asking ChatGPT to design one.
- **Add one new image, JOURNAL-05**, generated after JOURNAL-04 in the same
  conversation: the saved state from a neutral origin (Home or Journal) that
  shows the full in-the-moment **Use This Moment** share options plus an audience
  choice. This is the "catch the moment" screen.

AFTER JOURNAL-04 IS APPROVED, IMAGE JOURNAL-05 - MOMENT SAVED WITH IN-THE-MOMENT
OPTIONS, DESKTOP

Keep the exact accepted PeerSlate shell, palette, typographic voice, component
geometry, spacing, lighting, and restraint from JOURNAL-01 through JOURNAL-04.
This is the same product, one frame later — not a redesign.

Create a 16:10 widescreen frame, approximately 1440 by 900. Show the same
context-preserving composer completing over a neutral origin (the Journal or a
calm Home), with that origin still present and unchanged behind a contained
sheet or dialog. This is the moment right after a successful private save.

Dominant object: a calm "saved" confirmation that also lets the member act on
the moment while it is fresh.

Show this hierarchy, top to bottom:

- Moment saved privately - It is now in your Journal, with a quiet lock/privacy
  cue that does not rely on color alone. This save is automatic and already
  done.
- A short heading like Use This Moment offering four clearly optional,
  equally-weighted destination actions as calm chips or buttons: Share to Feed,
  Add to My Story, Use in Work, Add to Résumé. They are invitations, not a
  required next step.
- An audience row: Who can see it, showing Only you as the current default in a
  small selector. Audience is a separate, deliberate control.
- A quiet reassurance line: You will preview what is shared before anything
  posts.
- Done as the dominant completion action, with a quiet Back to page / close so
  the member can dismiss everything and stay where they were.

Truth and restraint requirements:

- The Moment is saved private to the Journal first; nothing on this screen is
  required for that.
- No option may look already-applied, pre-selected, toggled on, or auto-shared.
- No automatic publication, Feed post, Story insertion, résumé change, or
  audience broadening is shown.
- Any destination not yet available may appear only as a clearly disabled
  Coming later chip; never as a live control that implies it works.
- Keep a clear vertical reading order that could reflow accessibly; one dominant
  object, not a grid of equal cards.
- Add the same outside-the-frame annotation used on the other images:
  Production-intent concept - Journal is not currently live.

Do not show: an Add to Journal step; a Capture page or selected Capture tab;
social engagement counts; public/profile controls as if live; automatic AI
summaries presented as fact; a permanent navigation design.
