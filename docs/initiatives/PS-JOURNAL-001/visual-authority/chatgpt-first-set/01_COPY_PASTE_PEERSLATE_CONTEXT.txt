STATUS AND PURPOSE

PeerSlate is a multi-user, private-first professional memory, growth, and
expression platform. This assignment creates design reference images for a
future Journal experience. It is not a claim that Journal is implemented,
deployed, or live. Use generic fictional member data. Do not use Pete's real
employers, dates, biography, photos, or other personal information.

The governing product loop is:

Capture anywhere -> Save one private Moment -> find it immediately in the one
Journal -> optionally use that same Moment elsewhere now or later through a
governed reference.

LOCKED PRODUCT RULES

1. Capture is an action, not a place.
   - It may open from any eligible signed-in room.
   - It is not a page, tab, room, or required navigation destination.
   - The current page remains visible behind or around the composer where the
     form factor permits.
   - Opening and closing the composer preserves the member's origin context.

2. The universal composer has Type and Speak as equal first-class choices.
   - Neither choice is hidden behind the other.
   - Text remains usable if microphone, speech, AI, or another provider is
     unavailable.
   - Later media inputs may join the same architecture, but this visual round
     does not imply that every media type is live.

3. Save Moment is the single member-facing commit.
   - The successful action is labeled exactly: Save Moment.
   - It creates exactly one private canonical Moment in the member's own
     language.
   - That Moment becomes part of the owner's Journal by definition.
   - There is no Add to Journal step, draft-promotion journey, destination
     picker before writing, or forced trip to Journal after saving.
   - AI never delays, replaces, or silently rewrites the member's saved words.

4. The owner has one Journal.
   - It is the complete private chronological, searchable, correctable,
     exportable, deletable, and reusable record of eligible saved Moments.
   - Journal membership is derived from the canonical Moments. There is no
     copied Journal entry body and no separate Journal fact store.
   - Timeline, manage/list, detail, archive, and curation are local modes of
     the same Journal, not additional top-level products.

5. Journal and My Story are one truth system but different experiences.
   - Journal answers: What have I saved, how has it changed, who can see it,
     and what can I do with it?
   - My Story answers: How do I want someone to understand a selected set of
     experiences together?
   - Journal is complete, chronological, searchable, editable, and lifecycle
     oriented.
   - My Story is finite, intentionally selected, authored, cinematic, and
     visually composed for a purpose or audience.
   - Saving a Moment never adds it to My Story automatically.
   - Removing an item from My Story never removes its Moment from Journal.
   - Journal may reach My Story's level of editorial quality, media craft,
     chapter rhythm, and emotional care without becoming a drag-and-drop
     canvas or a prettier activity feed.

6. Privacy, audience, and publication are explicit.
   - New Moments default to Private to you / Only Me.
   - Save Moment does not publish, share, message, post to Feed, change a
     resume, alter My Story, create a Project, or broaden an audience.
   - The owner sees the complete Journal.
   - Selected-person, Connection, signed-in member, and Public views are later
     owner-curated, server-authorized projections of permitted fields only.
   - Authorization happens before data retrieval. A browser never downloads a
     private Journal and hides rows client-side.
   - Do not visually imply that public Journal, Connection views, or sharing
     are currently live in this first-round concept.

7. Reuse happens after save.
   - A saved state may offer View in Journal and Use This Moment as optional
     next actions.
   - The member can simply finish and remain in the origin room.
   - Story, Work, resume, Project, Board, Studio, Feed, public Journal, and
     messaging consume governed references later; they do not create a second
     canonical fact pipeline.

8. Navigation remains open.
   - Do not invent or finalize a permanent desktop or mobile navigation map.
   - Do not add a Capture tab or a second navigation layer.
   - A quiet Capture a Moment control may appear in the shell as an action, but
     it must not read as a route or destination.
   - Timeline, Manage, and similar Journal controls must read as local view
     controls, not global navigation.
