## Comparison result

### Already absorbed into Bible v2.6

The current Bible did not lose the entire v1.5.1 direction. It already carries:

- PeerSlate as a member-owned professional memory and expression system;
- one connected Slate and create-once/place-many;
- private-first Capture, review, confirmed Moment, and deliberate projection;
- Use This Moment as the first activation slice;
- What PeerSlate noticed, Slate Mirror, and Slate Replay;
- source-linked, uncertain, correctable Member Intelligence;
- deterministic authorization, privacy, publication, deletion, and state
  controls;
- a finite Home and intelligence embedded in existing domains; and
- the approved Journal/My Slate visual baseline.

The issue was therefore not wholesale loss. It was that the operational hold
prevented the Journal from becoming the actual member-facing spine that those
capabilities require.

### Reasserted and strengthened by the July 20 decision

The following are now explicit current implementation requirements:

1. **Journal is the member's memory profile**, not merely a destination in a
   Placement picker.
2. **Journal is chronological and private-first**, with a permission-filtered
   view of the same connected Slate for permitted viewers.
3. **Memory Intelligence belongs to that longitudinal member context.** It may
   appear in Home and Journal, but remains a separate private interpretation
   record until the member takes an explicit action.
4. **Activation closes the loop.** Use This Moment turns a confirmed Moment or
   approved insight into a useful next action through references, placements,
   or new private drafts—not copied facts.
5. **The Journal precedes broad intelligence implementation.** A person must be
   able to find, inspect, correct, archive, export, and delete their memory
   before the system claims to understand patterns across it.

### Superseded details that must not return

- v1.5.1's Iris Foundry styling is superseded by Deep Navy Gold and the July 18
  approved owner boards.
- v1.5.1 language that can be read as creating a second authoritative Journal
  record is narrowed by the deployed architecture: Capture preserves source;
  confirmed Moment is canonical; Placement/content links reference it; Journal
  is the governed memory-profile experience over that truth.
- The v1.5.1 package sequence is historical. Current delivered identity,
  Capture lifecycle, Moment, Placement, Voice, Photo flag-off, and Owner Home
  foundations must be reused rather than rebuilt.
- Public/profile audience work may not precede the shared server-side viewer
  authorization and payload-isolation gate.
- FitSlate remains tabled.

## Operational effect of this activation

their dates and are not rewritten. Current operational documents must say the
hold was lifted on July 20. Roadmap v2.5's embedded hold remains historical
sequence text; this owner decision and the current baseline supersede that
single status while retaining the Roadmap's Phase 5 requirements and gates.
