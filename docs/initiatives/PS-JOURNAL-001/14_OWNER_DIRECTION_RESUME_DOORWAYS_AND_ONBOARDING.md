# Owner Direction — Résumé Doorways, Onboarding, and Intent Entry (game plan)

**Recorded:** 2026-07-21, from Pete's direction. **Status:** direction/game-plan
record only — NOT an active package, no implementation authorized by this doc.
Sequenced after Journal J1 ships (every idea below is powered by the
composer + Moment engine).

## The owner's ask (plain)

The résumé/Work section feels slightly disconnected — acceptable, since Work is
a standout "where your work lives" showcase — but members need: a ~20-minute
onboarding; the ability to update things directly (add a picture, add a résumé
bullet) without only talking/texting into it, with everything archived into
their knowledge base (the Journal); and easy intent entry — "I just want to
work on my résumé today / do interview stuff" must be very accessible.

## The game plan — three doorways into existing machinery

### 1. Intent cards on the signed-in Home
"What do you want to do today?" — 3–4 warm cards: Work on my résumé · Practice
interviews · Capture a moment · Read my Journal. Fits the Bible's finite-Home
mandate; coordinates with the activated PS-HOME-FRONTEND-001 lane. One click =
in the mode; no nav hunting.

### 2. Résumé Studio (candidate package `PS-RESUME-STUDIO-001`)
The résumé opened in improve mode:
- **Direct add:** "Add a bullet" — member types/speaks the bullet; underneath
  it is one Save Moment (kind achievement/update) + an approved résumé
  placement in a single flow. One action for the member; canonical Moment +
  projection for the system. `PS-JRN-USE-004` is honored — nothing bypasses
  Save Moment; the composer's purpose hint (`PS-JRN-CAP-004`) carries the
  résumé intent. "Add a picture" = the composer's attachment row with a
  Work/résumé-evidence purpose hint.
- **Gap prompts:** deterministic prompts from the résumé's own holes ("this
  role has no metric") answered by voice or type; each answer = Moment +
  suggested bullet the member approves. Improving the résumé = capturing
  moments; the loop feeds the Journal.
- All placements previewed and approved; nothing auto-publishes; AI proposes
  only. Existing rules unchanged.

### 3. Guided ~20-minute onboarding (candidate package `PS-ONBOARD-001`)
A conversational first session, Speak and Type first-class (voice stack is
released): roles → three wins per role → numbers → skills. Output: a working
résumé AND a seeded private Journal in the first sitting — day-one proof of
capture-once-use-everywhere. Resumable, progress-visible, skippable, never a
gate to the rest of the product. Highest-leverage unbuilt experience for a new
member's first session.

### Work ↔ Résumé relationship (owner ruling)
Work stays a distinct standout showcase; the résumé stays the document. They
share the same Moments and connect through flow (Use This Moment chips, links,
gap prompts) — they are not merged. The slight separateness is intentional.

## Sequencing

1. Journal J1 (in flight) → J1.1 search (in flight) → pilot.
2. Then these become flagship Phase 5–6 packages: PS-ONBOARD-001 and
   PS-RESUME-STUDIO-001, each with its own manager/writer, visual authority,
   and owner acceptance per the standards. Intent cards ride with the Owner
   Home frontend lane's sequencing.
3. Nothing here modifies current routes, fixtures, or behavior today.
