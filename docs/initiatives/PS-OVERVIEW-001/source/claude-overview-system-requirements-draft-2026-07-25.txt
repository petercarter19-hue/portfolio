# Member Overview — System Requirements Draft

**Working package name:** `PS-OVERVIEW-001`
**Status:** Draft requirements for owner review. Not a visual authority. Not implementation authorization.
**Visual authority:** The Pete overview composition (owner-supplied image, 2026-07-25). Exact file/hash lock pending.
**Scope:** The member-configurable Overview band that renders above the full résumé on a public Slate.
**Out of scope:** The full résumé itself, Story, Work, Slate Board, Interview Studio.

---

## 0. The governing principle

> **A member never edits a layout. A member fills a content model. The layout is computed from what they filled.**

Every requirement below exists to serve that sentence. The moment a member can drag a box or set a
width, the design stops being able to guarantee it looks right, and "weird gaps" become
unavoidable. Instead: the member chooses *what to say*, and the system deterministically decides
*how it renders* from the volume and type of what they said.

This also satisfies the existing canonical-truth rule. The Overview is a **projection**, not a
second truth store.

---

## 1. Content model

### 1.1 Two card families

| Family | Source of content | Member action | Can it be empty? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Derived** | Canonical résumé records | Select which surface, and their order | No — it renders only if canonical records exist |
| **Authored** | Member-written text (optionally AI-drafted) | Write, edit, approve, publish | Yes — an unfilled authored card simply does not render |

**Derived cards never duplicate a fact.** The Education card in the Overview and the Education
section in the résumé are the same records rendered twice. Editing either edits the canonical
record. There is no second copy and no sync problem.

This single decision eliminates most empty-state risk: a member with no certifications cannot
produce an empty Certifications card, because the card's existence is a function of the data.

### 1.2 Card inventory

**Rail (left column) — stacked, order configurable within rules**

| # | Card | Family | Canonical source | Min to render | Max shown | Overflow |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Executive Brief | Authored | — | 40 chars | 320 chars | none (hard cap) |
| 2 | Career Snapshot | Derived | roles | 1 role | 4 roles | `View full experience →` |
| 3 | **Skills** *(was Core Tools)* | Derived | skills | 3 skills | 12 skills | `View all N skills →` |
| 4 | Education | Derived | education | 1 entry | 4 entries | `View all N degrees →` |
| 5 | Certifications | Derived | certifications | 1 entry | 4 entries | `View all N certifications →` |
| 6 | Awards | Derived | awards | 1 entry | 4 entries | `View all N awards →` |
| 7 | Custom list | Authored | — | 2 items | 5 items | none |

Order reflects the owner's 2026-07-25 correction: **Skills above Education; Certifications and
Awards below both.** A member may reorder, but the Executive Brief is pinned first.

**Bands (right column) — full-width rows, member picks 0–6 from a library**

| Band type | Suggested purpose | Media slot |
|---|---|---|
| Discipline band | A domain of expertise (e.g. "Systems Engineering & Requirements") | optional |
| Leadership band | Scope of people/program responsibility | optional |
| Outcomes band | 2–8 metric tiles, no prose | none |
| Lifecycle / specialty band | A named specialty (e.g. "Sustainment & Lifecycle") | optional |
| Emerging-work band | What they're learning or applying now | optional |
| Personal band | "The person behind the work" | optional |
| Forward band | "What I'm building toward" | optional |
| Custom band | Member-titled | optional |

**Every band is optional.** A member may publish an Overview with zero bands. Band types are
templates for prompting and media behavior, not required sections.

**Stat strip (below hero) — 0, or 2 to 4 items**

| Field | Limit |
|---|---|
| Value | 12 chars (`9 / $19.2M` = 10) |
| Label | 28 chars |
| Icon | chosen from a fixed set, or omitted |
| Source link | optional; points at the canonical record supporting the number |

## 2. Degradation rules — how gaps are prevented

This is the core of the spec. Each rule is a hard product rule, not a styling preference.

### R1 — Nothing renders below its minimum

A card with less than its `min to render` does not render as a card. It is either merged (R2) or
omitted. **The system never pads a thin card to fill space.**

### R2 — Collapse and merge, never stretch

When credential-type cards fall below minimum individually, they merge:

| Condition | Result |
|---|---|
| Education = 1 entry AND Certifications ≤ 2 | Single card: **Education & Certifications** |
| Certifications ≤ 2 AND Awards ≤ 2 | Single card: **Credentials** |
| All three thin | Single card: **Credentials & Education** |
| Any one is rich (≥3) | It stands alone; the others may still merge |

A member with one bachelor's degree and one cert gets one tidy card, not two stubs and a gap.

### R3 — The stat strip is 0, 2, 3, or 4 — never 1, never 5+

- 0 items → the strip does not render; the hero flows directly into the body
- 2 → halves; 3 → thirds; 4 → quarters (equal distribution, no orphan cell)
- A member may enter at most 4. A 5th requires removing one.
- 1 item is not permitted; the editor prompts for a second or offers to move it into an Outcomes band

### R4 — Metric grids reduce columns rather than orphan a row

Outcomes bands hold 2–8 tiles. Column count is chosen so the **final row is at least half full**.

| Tiles | Columns | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 4 | 4 |
| 5 | 3 | 3 + 2 |
| 6 | 3 | 3 + 3 |
| 7 | 4 | 4 + 3 |
| 8 | 4 | 4 + 4 |

Never 4 + 1.

### R5 — Bands with no media pair up

A band with a media slot filled renders full-width (text left, image right — the locked
composition). A band with **no media** renders as a text block. Two consecutive text-only bands
render as a **two-column row**. Three consecutive text-only bands render 2-up then 1 full-width.

This prevents a stack of short, image-less bands reading as sparse.

### R6 — The rail does not stretch to match the bands

The rail and the band column are independent flows aligned to the top. When the rail ends first,
it ends — optionally becoming sticky. **No rule may stretch, center, or pad the rail to match
column height.** This must be stated so a later change does not "fix" it.

### R7 — Layout variant is selected by content volume, not by the member

| Variant | Requires | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Full** | ≥2 stats, ≥3 rail cards, ≥3 bands | The locked composition: hero + strip + rail + bands |
| **B — Compact** | ≥1 rail card, ≥1 band | Hero + optional strip + bands full-width + one horizontal credentials row |
| **C — Starter** | Any published profile | Hero + Executive Brief + Career Snapshot + a truthful "your Overview grows as you add" state |

The system selects the richest variant the content supports. The member can preview the others and
sees exactly what is needed to unlock the next one ("Add one more band to use the Full layout").
**A member cannot select a variant their content would break.**

### R8 — Overflow becomes a specific link, never a silent trim

Content over a card's `max shown` is not cut off. The card shows its first N and renders a
specific link: **`View all 6 skills →`**, not `More`. The remainder lives in the résumé below.

- The link renders only if the target section exists **and** holds more than the card shows
- If the target is absent or equal, the card renders with no link rather than a link to nothing

### R9 — Authored text over hard cap blocks publish, not typing

The editor never blocks keystrokes. It shows live state: **Fits** / **Will show 3 of 6 — a
"View all" link will appear** / **Too long — trim N characters to publish.** Publish is blocked
only in the third case.

## 3. Deep-link contract

Every derived card's link targets the corresponding résumé section.

- Target sections carry stable anchors (`#education`, `#skills`, `#certifications`, `#awards`, `#experience`)
- Activating a link moves **focus** to the target section heading, not just scroll position
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` → instant jump, no smooth scroll
- Links are real anchors — they work with middle-click, keyboard, and JS disabled
- If a member has hidden the target résumé section, the Overview card renders without a link

## 4. Editing model

### 4.1 Owner-only affordances

- Edit state is **server-derived** from the session. A client flag never grants edit capability.
- Public render and owner render use **the same view model**; the owner payload adds `editable: true` per slot.
- Edit controls are real `<button>` elements in the tab order. **Hover-only reveal is not permitted** —
  reveal on hover *and* on focus, and keep them reachable on touch.
- A persistent **Preview as visitor** toggle shows the exact audience result, including which cards
  drop out at the visitor's audience level.

### 4.2 Draft, preview, publish

Editing the Overview does **not** change the public page. Three explicit states:

1. **Draft** — private, autosaved, unlimited iteration
2. **Preview** — exact rendered audience result, including the selected variant
3. **Publish** — explicit action; publishes the whole Overview atomically

Rationale: a half-edited Overview must never be the public page. Matches the existing Story
composition standard (layout save and publication are separate explicit actions).

### 4.3 Reordering

- Rail cards and bands are reorderable by drag **and** by an equivalent keyboard/structured control
  (move up / move down / move to position N)
- The Executive Brief is pinned first
- Default order follows §1.2; a member may deviate

### 4.4 Per-slot controls

Every slot offers: **Edit**, **Hide from Overview** (keeps the canonical record), **Revert to canonical**,
and for authored slots, **Clear**.

## 5. AI model

Both paths are first-class. Neither is the default.

### 5.1 What AI may do

- Draft an Executive Brief from the member's canonical records
- Draft a band title and body from a selected role, project, or skill cluster
- **Propose which bands to use** based on what the résumé contains ("Your history is heavy on
  sustainment — want a Sustainment band?")
- Suggest which skills to surface in the rail
- Rewrite for length when a slot is over its cap

### 5.2 What AI may never do

- **Fill a stat value.** Every number is typed by the member or pulled from a canonical record.
  A fabricated `$36M+` is a real-world harm to a real person's credibility.
- Save, publish, or change audience
- Overwrite an existing authored slot without the member accepting the replacement
- Invent an award, degree, certification, employer, date, or title

### 5.3 How a draft behaves

An AI draft lands **in the editor as editable text**, visibly marked as a draft, with the canonical
records it drew from listed and inspectable. The member edits, accepts, or discards. Accepting
converts it to member-authored content. Nothing is saved until the member saves.

## 6. Empty and partial states

| Situation | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Brand-new member, no résumé | Variant C. Honest prompt: what to add first, and what it will unlock. |
| Résumé but no authored content | Variant C or B from derived cards alone. Overview is usable with zero writing. |
| Bachelor's only, no certs/awards | R2 merge → one Credentials & Education card. |
| No quantified outcomes | No stat strip. Hero flows into body. |
| No images at all | R5 text-band pairing. Never an empty media box. |
| Member hides everything | Overview does not render; résumé is the first content on the page. |

---

## 7. Accessibility requirements

- DOM order matches visual reading order; no CSS reordering that breaks screen-reader sequence
- Each card is a labelled region with a heading; the rail is a `<nav>`-free list, not a menu
- Edit affordances reachable and operable by keyboard, with visible focus
- Deep links manage focus (§3)
- All state changes (draft saved, published, variant changed) announced politely
- Text scales to 200% without loss of content or function; bands reflow to single column
- Contrast meets WCAG 2.2 AA against both the light body and the navy strip

## 8. Open decisions for the owner

1. **Custom bands** — free-titled bands, or a fixed library only? Free titles risk quality drift.
2. **Band count cap** — 6 proposed. Higher risks the Overview becoming a second résumé.
3. **Stat source links** — should each stat be required to link to supporting evidence, or optional?
   Required is stronger and matches the évidence-linked résumé, but it raises the bar to publish.
4. **Overview visibility** — always public with the résumé, or independently audience-controlled?
5. **Variant override** — may a member force Variant B when their content supports A?
6. **Hero** — is the hero (photo, title, pull-quote) part of this system or separate profile chrome?

---

## 9. What this does not authorize

This is a requirements draft. It authorizes no route, schema, migration, flag, template, or
member-facing capability. It does not lock a visual authority. Implementation requires a bounded
package, an exact owner-locked visual, and an entry gate.
