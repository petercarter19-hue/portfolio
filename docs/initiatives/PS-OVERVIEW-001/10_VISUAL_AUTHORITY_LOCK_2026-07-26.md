## 10. Responsive and accessibility interpretation

- Mobile is recomposed, not a scaled-down desktop.
- Public Story & Career stacks media, identity, contact/actions, and proof.
- Desktop editor inspectors become full-width mobile sheets or focused steps.
- The left rail becomes a labeled Sections control/chip treatment.
- The right AI rail becomes a drawer/sheet or prominent contextual action.
- The AI rail undocks before the center becomes materially cramped.
- The implementation must preserve semantic order, keyboard operation, visible
  focus, touch targets, 200-percent reflow, reduced motion, forced colors, and
  no horizontal page scrolling.

## 11. Allowed implementation adaptations

Documented non-material adaptations are allowed for:

- semantic HTML and assistive-technology clarity;
- visible focus and touch targets;
- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast;
- reduced motion and forced colors;
- real text/localization reflow;
- count-aware and missing-content states;
- truthful media/destination failure;
- integration with stable résumé anchors; and
- shell collapse needed to preserve the accepted center composition.

A change to dominant composition, hierarchy, primary action, typography
family, color language, style identity, or responsive interaction model is
material and returns to ChatGPT visual creation and Pete.
