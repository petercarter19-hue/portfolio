text should visually read at roughly 16px or larger, with comfortable line
length; avoid tiny dense text.

Required composition:
1. A slim site header with PeerSlate/member identity at left and simple page
   navigation. Shared contextual controls appear once in the page chrome:
   “Download PDF” and “Ask Pete AI”. Do not duplicate these elsewhere.

3. A full-width editorial story band with a scenic mountain or lighthouse
   image and a compact card labeled “My Story”, headline “Purpose. People.

   usable.

5. A combined Impact + Skills band: four concise achievement tiles followed by
