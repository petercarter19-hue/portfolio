## 2. Product thesis

PeerSlate Community should be familiar in ten seconds and meaningfully
different after ten days.

The Feed is a finite, work-leading but whole-person stream where people share
what they are building, learning, solving, trying, finishing, questioning, and
living. Rollout prompts should establish work-casual behavior: what happened at
work, what someone just learned, what finally clicked, what they are trying,
or where they could use a hand. Outside-work posts remain welcome as normal
Community Posts.

A post may remain a small ordinary post forever. When something continues, the
author may add what happened next, acknowledge useful help, and show a simple
`Then -> Now` change. That voluntary continuity powers PeerSlate's distinctive
return loop.

The Break stays a separate Community destination, but it also supplies changing
programming around the Feed. In places where LinkedIn or Facebook would insert
an advertisement, PeerSlate can insert a reset, question, small challenge,
member feature, local discovery item, or another clearly labeled Break module.
The Feed remains about people; The Break changes the rhythm.

The private Home/profile is not part of this direction package. Community may
link to an authorized profile destination supplied by that separate system,
but this record makes no decision about its page, route, layout, sections,
viewer composition, or visual design.

## 3. Product principles

1. **Familiar first, distinctive through use.**
2. **Work-casual leads the culture; the whole person still belongs.**
3. **A post can grow, but it never has to.**
4. **Vertical means new events; horizontal means change through time.**
5. **The Feed is people; The Break changes the rhythm.**
6. **Return for something meaningful, not because the Feed never ends.**
7. **A restorative interruption can replace an advertisement slot.**
8. **A healthy exit is a successful session.**
9. **No response, update, outcome, or continued participation is required to
   make a post worthwhile.**
10. **Community does not absorb the separate Home/profile, Journal, Studio,
    Project, or Goal Board systems.**

## 5. Community surface roles

| Surface | Unique question it answers | Must not become |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community Feed | What are people sharing, learning, solving, questioning, and living now? | Infinite entertainment, resume directory, or content-marketing arena |
| Composer | What do I want to share, with whom, and what response would help? | Private Capture, Journal entry, or complicated publishing studio |
| Post card | What did this person say, who can see it, and how can I respond? | Popularity scoreboard or compressed profile page |
| In Motion | What is still moving, and what changed? | Generic carousel, trend shelf, or progress obligation |
| Since You Were Here | What directly deserves my attention after I was away? | Second Feed, reaction firehose, or popularity digest |
| The Break | What changes the rhythm today? | Outside-work feed or stale filler archive |
| From The Break | What brief programmed interruption is useful here? | Advertisement or disguised member activity |
| Community Pulse | What small amount of interesting Community movement should I notice? | Continuous global feed or permanent navigation system |
| Action rail | What reply, help, or thread change may need me? | Decorative furniture or engagement ranking |
| Thread detail | What was said, what followed, and what changed in this conversation? | Project workspace, private record, or forced outcome workflow |
| Caught-up landing | What is the healthiest useful next step? | Infinite refill mechanism |

## 6. Vertical and horizontal rule

### Vertical

An item belongs in the vertical stream when it can stand alone:

- a new post;
- a question;
- a work or life observation;
- a photo or short media item;
- a lesson;
- an author update that should appear as a new event;
- an outside-work moment; or
- a completed result.

Vertical answers: **What is happening across my Community?**

### Horizontal

Horizontal movement is allowed only for:

1. ordered change through time within the same thread; or
2. a bounded collection of active threads that each contain real change.

Horizontal answers: **What is still moving, and what changed?**

Do not use horizontal movement merely for suggested people, random quotes,
trends, ads, inspirational prompts, or another feed.

## 7. Signature return loop

The approved return loop combines familiar social behavior with a PeerSlate
continuity layer:

```text
Share what happened
  -> receive a meaningful response (optional)
  -> add what happened next (optional)
  -> show Then -> Now or acknowledge what helped (optional)
  -> resurface as In Motion or Since You Were Here
  -> close, pause, keep open, or simply leave it as an ordinary post
```

Three parts make it distinctive:

1. **Meaningful re-entry:** `Since You Were Here` prioritizes a reply, offer of
   help, or change in a thread the member touched.
2. **Visible change:** `In Motion` shows evolving threads with a concise
   `Then -> Now`, not merely popular content.
3. **Calm completion:** the caught-up boundary offers one deliberate next move
   and then permits the member to leave.

No unresolved thread is a failure. PeerSlate may ask `What happened next?` at
natural boundaries, but the author can dismiss, pause, complete, or ignore it.
There are no streaks, unresolved-thread nags, or `people are waiting` pressure.

## 8. Break programming model

Candidate rotating modules include:

- today's reset;
- Mood Switch;
- a thoughtful question;
- a real poll with a defined purpose and audience;
- a concise quote with truthful source and curation label;
- a short optional challenge;
- local discovery with real sourcing and location truth;
- a member feature;
- an outside-work member feature;
- something to try;
- something surprising, funny, or restorative; and
- a prompt that becomes useful only after a real member answers it.

Distribution rules:

- The complete current edition lives in The Break.
- One selection normally appears inside a Feed session; a second is allowed
  only in a longer or caught-up session.
- One quiet selection may appear in a Community rail when it is not repeated
  nearby.
- Programming changes daily or on another truthfully published cadence.
- Expired programming does not pretend to be current.
- Member features use the original authorized post or reference.
- No slot fabricates popularity, participation, a vote, a location,
  recommendation, community density, or advertiser.
- Members can dismiss or reduce Break programming without losing Community.

## 10. Measurement

### Meaningful continuity

- author update;
- helpful acknowledgement;
- question voluntarily marked helped, resolved, paused, or closed;
- real adaptation/outcome link; and
- participant return to a meaningful update.

### Personal usefulness

- private Saves reopened;
- posts deliberately connected to later governed objects;
- member-rated session usefulness;
- member-rated audience confidence;
- useful sessions with zero posting; and
- value reported on posts with zero responses.

### Trust and healthy use

- unauthorized-visibility incidents;
- audience changes immediately after posting;
- deletion, block, report, and mute rates;
- first substantive posts receiving a thoughtful response;
- sessions reaching caught-up;
- notification opt-down;
- Break dismiss/reduce rates; and
- qualitative reports of pressure, interruption, or obligation.

Time spent, posts per day, session depth, reaction totals, or public influence
are not primary success measures.

- autoplay or a Reels-style launch format;

- streaks, leaderboards, helping points, or achievement economies;
- fake trends, discovery, votes, locations, advertisers, or density;
- generic programming disguised as member activity;
- open cold direct messaging;
- forced Project, skill, private-record, Moment, or Journal selection;
- automatic Journal ingestion or profile publication;
- duplicate post records across Feed and The Break;
- AI-written posts presented as the member's unaided voice;
- guilt, urgency, unresolved-thread nags, or `behind` warnings;
- the same Break item in the center and both rails;
- a Community mini-feed silently inserted into every focused workspace;
- a new permanent navigation layer without route/shell authority; or
- retirement or redesign of the Goal Board through this initiative.
