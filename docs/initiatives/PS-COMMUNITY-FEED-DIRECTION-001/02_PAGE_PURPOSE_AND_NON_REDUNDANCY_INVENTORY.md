- **Pete inventory approval / date:** Approved / 2026-07-30

### External profile boundary

Feed items may link to an authorized member profile supplied by the separate
Home/profile system. This inventory does not define, redesign, implement, or
create visual authority for that page. It does not decide what a member sees on
their own Home, what another viewer sees, or where Community activity appears
outside Community.
