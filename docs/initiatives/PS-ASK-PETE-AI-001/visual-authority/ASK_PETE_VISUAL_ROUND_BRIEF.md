# Ask Pete AI — Visual Creation Round

Status: visual direction only. No production implementation, route change, enablement, or deployment is authorized by this artifact.

## Product moment to prove

A recruiter selects **Give me Pete's 60-second recruiter brief** and receives, without losing the resume:

1. A concise professional through-line.
2. Three consequential, evidence-backed claims.
3. Inspectable claim-level citations.
4. An honest **Not established publicly** boundary.
5. Two useful interview questions.
6. A direct route to Pete when a human answer matters.

The product promise is: **Ask Pete answers when it can, shows its evidence, admits when it cannot, and creates a direct path to Pete when the human answer matters.**

## Fixed boundaries

- Preserve the resume's content structure and make the resume readable without AI.
- Keep one canonical Ask Pete conversation across every entry point.
- Ask Pete speaks about Pete, never as Pete.
- AI output is synthesis; Pete-approved evidence remains the authority.
- Contextual actions prefill an editable question and never auto-submit.
- The human handoff opens existing contact options in this slice; it does not imply an inbox, delivery, or automatic knowledge update.
- Do not use candidate-fit scores.

## Visual strategy

- Replace the cold blue field with warm ivory, soft sage/eucalyptus, deep forest ink, and a restrained amber/bronze accent.
- Preserve the existing warm portrait and landscape imagery.
- Use one coherent depth language: warm surfaces, fine borders, restrained neutral shadows, and a clear foreground/midground/background hierarchy.
- Avoid card soup, glassmorphism, decorative AI glow, heavy gradients, and a dark dominant rail.
- Use color by role rather than decoration: neutral field, forest interaction color, warm accent for evidence/attention, and explicit icons plus labels for support states.
- Accessibility remains part of the visual lock: readable contrast, non-color state cues, obvious keyboard focus, no obscured focused content, and responsive reflow.

## Required desktop states

### 1. Recruiter entry / capability preview

- Strong hero action: **Ask Pete**.
- Canonical right rail or integrated companion surface.
- Dominant starter: **Give me Pete's 60-second recruiter brief**.
- Empty state previews a real citation unit, not just a promise:
  - **Supported** label.
  - Exact evidence excerpt.
  - Role/source identity.
  - **Open on resume** action.
- Compact trust statement: **Answers use information Pete approved for Ask Pete.**

### 2. Answer state

- Short recruiter brief.
- Three claim blocks with exact evidence connections.
- **Used in this answer: 4 public records — Show all on resume.**
- Honest boundary: **Not established in Pete's approved public information.**
- Two interview questions with a short explanation of why each matters.
- **Ask Pete directly** next step.

### 3. Source-open coordination

- Conversation remains visible.
- Center resume scrolls to the exact evidence unit.
- Role chapter/evidence opens and receives a restrained temporary highlight.
- Rail shows current context, for example: **Skills → MBSE · 3 approved source records**.

## Responsive direction

- Wide desktop: persistent 380–420 px evidence-companion rail.
- Narrow desktop/tablet: dismissible side sheet preserving the same conversation.
- Mobile: bottom sheet with explicit close control and a compact persistent Ask Pete trigger.
- Opening/closing returns focus appropriately and never covers the focused element without a way to reveal it.

## First-round concepts

### A. Warm Sage Evidence Companion — recommended

Warm ivory center, pale sage outer field, deep forest actions, muted bronze evidence accents. Editorial and human, with a persistent evidence rail that feels native to the resume.

### B. Eucalyptus Recruiter Desk

Warm white and eucalyptus surfaces, a slightly more product-forward rail, tighter recruiter workflow, and clearer tool/status language. Technologically capable without becoming a chatbot dashboard.

### C. Mineral Moss Quiet Tech

Sand and mineral neutrals with moss/olive structure and a restrained clay accent. The most distinctive and design-forward option, with careful controls to avoid reducing readability.
