## Binding interpretation

- The initial authorized author signs in. Identity and owner-pilot eligibility
  are server-derived.
- A draft is private and opening the composer creates no public record.
- `Public` must be deliberately selected and confirmed for each publication;
  public is not inferred or silently remembered.
- Signed-out visitors can read published-public posts and no private/member
  payload. They cannot author or interact in the first owner pilot.
- Public audience changes after publication, broader registration, direct
  messaging, AI publishing, member-only/Connections/selected-person audiences,
  and automatic Journal/Slate creation remain outside this lock.

The six PNGs are byte-for-byte copies of the accepted candidate set. Any
material change requires a new ChatGPT-created candidate and Pete's new exact
lock.
