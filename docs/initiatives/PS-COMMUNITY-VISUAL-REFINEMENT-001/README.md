composer are too green-heavy and visually flat. Preserve the accepted compact,
efficient composer composition while revising its color language before any
runtime implementation.

green room and not a rainbow interface. The composer becomes a clean white
creative surface. Deep green remains a PeerSlate brand anchor, while a small
semantic accent family gives media, voice, AI, Community signals, and supporting
