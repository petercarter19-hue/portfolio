--- TOPIC: Widget HTML explanation ---

PETE: what is the widget html that i need to add? explain.

CLAUDE: Explained the two parts of the chat widget:

Part 1 — Floating button: Round button, bottom-right corner. Click opens the
chat box. Click again, closes it.

Part 2 — Chat box panel: Three sections:
  - Header ("Ask about Pete")
  - Message area (scrollable conversation)
  - Input bar (text field + Send button)

Where it goes: Right before </body> in base.html. Goes there because base.html
is the shared layout — adding it once puts it on every page automatically.

HTML provided:
  <button id="chat-toggle" aria-label="Open chat assistant">
      <span id="chat-icon-open">💬</span>
      <span id="chat-icon-close">✕</span>
  </button>
  <div id="chat-box">
      <div id="chat-header"><span>Ask about Pete</span></div>
      <div id="chat-messages">
          <div class="chat-message bot-message">
              Hi! I'm Pete's AI assistant. Ask me anything about his
              background, experience, or skills.
          </div>
      </div>
      <div id="chat-input-area">
          <input type="text" id="chat-input" placeholder="Ask a question..." />
          <button id="chat-send">Send</button>
      </div>
  </div>
  <script src="{{ url_for('static', filename='js/chatbot.js') }}"></script>
