The problem

Meaningful work and life disappear between formal milestones.

The breakthrough on a Tuesday afternoon. The hard conversation you handled well. The skill you finally understood. None of it fits on a résumé line, so it gets lost—and when you need it most, you are reconstructing your own story from memory.

The living Journal

One record that compounds.

The Journal is your timeline—work and life, finished and unfinished. Everything you capture lives there once and connects everywhere it matters: your Story, your Work, your résumé, your goals. Enter once. Link everywhere. Publish deliberately.

How It Works

Capture. Understand. Choose. Compound.

  1. 1

    Capture something real

    Say what happened—by voice or typing. It starts as a private draft, always.

  2. 2

    Let PeerSlate understand it

    AI proposes a title, connections, and context. Proposals stay proposals until you approve them.

  3. 3

    Choose what it becomes

    Keep it private, share it with people you choose, or publish it. You set the audience every time.

  4. 4

    Get more from it later

    Interview prep, a tailored résumé, your Story—drawn from the record you already built.

AI, on your side

AI helps. It never takes over.

PeerSlate's AI clarifies, connects, and prepares—grounded only in the history you have approved. It never publishes for you, never widens an audience, never invents an accomplishment, and never converts your voice into corporate performance copy. Every AI output is a draft until you accept it.

Private first

Yours by default. Public by decision.

Everything new is private. Sharing is a deliberate act with a clear audience—yourself, selected people, connections, community, or public. You can inspect, correct, export, archive, or delete anything you own, any time.

Plainly

What PeerSlate is—and is not.

A living record of your work and growth, with a community built around real progress. Not an engagement casino, a follower economy, a job board, or a place where AI performs your life for you. No trending tabs, no popularity scores, no filler.
