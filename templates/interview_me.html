      STAR Coach
      • S
        SituationSet the context. What was the challenge you faced?
      • T
        TaskWhat was your responsibility or goal?
      • A
        ActionWhat did you actually do? Be specific.
      • R
        ResultWhat changed? Lead with metrics and impact.
      Interview Tips
      • Lead with the result.Say the outcome early, then explain how you got there.
      • Use numbers.A metric ("70% faster", "$4.6M delivered") beats an adjective every time.
      • Keep it under 2 minutes.Tight answers signal clear thinking. Practice trimming.
      • Say "I", not "we".Interviewers want your role — name what you personally did.
      • End on impact.Close with what changed for the team, customer, or mission.
