MY JOURNEY

A Path Built on
Curiosity and Grit

My path into engineering was anything but traditional. I earned my electrical engineering degree as a non-traditional student, then began my career as a Department of Defense systems engineer sustaining Air Force avionics across seven aircraft platforms — leading special projects, a $36M engineering-services contract, and a flight of 30 engineers along the way. I completed a master's in engineering management while working full time, deepened my reliability and MBSE experience at L3Harris, and today I define and trace system requirements for Northrop Grumman's Sentinel program. Every step has sharpened how I bring structure to complex problems and align people toward results that matter.
